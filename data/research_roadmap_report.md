### A · 大动物基因编辑与疾病模型

**中文**
- **进展：** CRISPR/Cas9 + 体细胞核移植（SCNT）在猪上已是成熟流水线；从单基因敲除走向精准敲入、碱基编辑（ABE/CBE）、prime editing。多位点编辑猪（去除免疫原/PERV）已推动猪心、猪肾移植进入人体实质阶段（2022 年以来）。
- **热点：** 异种器官移植；猪作为药代与免疫模型；编辑动物的遗传稳定性与生物安全评估；无病毒载体递送。
- **空白：** 大动物脱靶与嵌合体评估无统一标准；疾病模型普遍「建成了但机制没深挖」；成本与世代周期使机制迭代困难；缺少大动物的单细胞/空间图谱。
- **趋势：** **模型工程本身正在服务化、商品化**——做出一个模型的学术溢价在持续下降，价值转移到「用模型回答机制问题」和「作为转化终点的验证」。
- **对你的含义：** 这条线技术含量高、工业界可读性强，但**作为独立 PI 主线已经偏晚**。应定位为工具与终点验证，不作主打。

**English** — Pig CRISPR+SCNT is a mature pipeline; the frontier has moved to precise knock-in, base and prime editing, and multiplex-edited donor pigs now in human xenotransplant trials. Open problems: no standardised off-target/mosaicism assessment in large animals, phenotype-only disease models with shallow mechanism, prohibitive cost per iteration, missing single-cell atlases. The trend that matters for you: **model building itself is commoditising**, so the academic premium has shifted from making a model to answering a mechanistic question with one. Position this as a tool and a translational endpoint, not as your independent line.

### B · 代谢×病毒复制（胆固醇 / 乳酸）

**中文**
- **进展：** 病毒对宿主脂质与胆固醇通路的依赖（复制细胞器膜、脂筏进入）已确立；SREBP/HMGCR 轴、25-羟胆固醇/CH25H 抗病毒轴清楚；感染细胞的 Warburg 样重编程被广泛报道。
- **热点：** 宿主导向抗病毒药物；**乳酸与蛋白乳酰化（lactylation）作为新表观修饰**（2019 年后爆发式增长）；代谢-天然免疫交叉（cGAS/STING、RIG-I 的代谢调控）；脂滴生物学。
- **空白：** **乳酸/乳酰化在 RNA 层面的作用几乎空白**；代谢物如何调控 RNA 稳定性与翻译罕有系统研究；兽医病毒（CSFV/PEDV）的机制发现难以进入主流人类疾病语境。
- **趋势：** 从「代谢为病毒供料」走向「代谢物作为信号，通过乳酰化/巴豆酰化等修饰直接改写宿主机器」。
- **对你的含义：** 你的一作 #8（乳酸→胆固醇→病毒复制）是一条**完整的代谢物因果链**，方法学可以整体迁移到「代谢物→RNA 降解机器」。**但「兽医病毒」这个外壳在北美/欧洲经费体系里是负资产，必须把内核剥出来。**

**English** — Viral dependence on host lipid/cholesterol pathways is established (SREBP/HMGCR, CH25H/25-HC), as is Warburg-like rewiring in infected cells. Hot: host-directed antivirals, and **lactate/protein lactylation as a post-2019 modification field**. The gap that matters: **lactate and lactylation have almost no established role at the RNA level**, and metabolite control of RNA stability is barely studied. Your first-author lactate→cholesterol→replication chain is a complete metabolite-to-phenotype causal argument and transfers wholesale; but the "veterinary virus" packaging is a liability in US/EU funding — extract the core and drop the shell.

### C · 器官纤维化

**中文**
- **进展：** 共同通路清楚（TGF-β/SMAD、YAP/TAZ 机械转导、成纤维细胞→肌成纤维细胞转化）；单细胞图谱已定义病理性成纤维细胞亚群；但**获批抗纤维化药只有 nintedanib 与 pirfenidone，且仅限肺（IPF）——心、肠、肝纤维化零获批药**。
- **热点：** 纤维化的**可逆性与消退（resolution）**；成纤维细胞的代谢重编程（糖酵解/乳酸依赖）；机械力-代谢耦合；器官特异机制 vs 共同机制；类器官与 fibrosis-on-chip 药筛。
- **空白：** **肠纤维化（IBD 狭窄）机制与药物极度缺乏**；抗纤维化 miR-29 已知十余年却未成药——「递送」和「为什么内源 miR-29 在纤维化时被关掉」两个问题都没解；极少有实验室能在**同一套逻辑下横向比较心脏与肠道**纤维化。
- **趋势：** 从抑制活化转向促进消退；靶向成纤维细胞代谢；RNA 疗法（miRNA mimic / antagomiR / ASO）。
- **对你的含义：** **这是你现有资产里领域需求最强、你位置最扎实的一条**——两个不同器官的一作论文 + 自己当 PI 的基金。而「内源 miR-29 为什么被关掉」这个公认空白，恰好是你能用 RNA 降解机制去回答的。

**English** — Core pathways (TGF-β/SMAD, YAP/TAZ, myofibroblast conversion) and pathological fibroblast subsets are well mapped, yet **only two drugs are approved, both lung-only; cardiac, intestinal and hepatic fibrosis have none**. Hot: fibrosis resolution, fibroblast metabolic rewiring, mechano-metabolic coupling, organoid/organ-chip screening. Key gaps: intestinal fibrosis (IBD strictures) is mechanistically and therapeutically bare; **anti-fibrotic miR-29 has been known for over a decade without becoming a drug — neither delivery nor "why is endogenous miR-29 switched off in fibrosis" is solved**; and almost no lab compares heart and gut fibrosis under one framework. This is the strongest-demand, best-owned position among your existing assets — and that open question is exactly one an RNA-decay mechanism can answer.

### E · 类器官与碱基编辑平台

**中文**
- **进展：** 类器官已从肠道扩展到几乎全器官；类器官生物库的药敏结果已有前瞻性临床相关数据；碱基编辑已进入临床（心血管、血液病方向）。
- **热点：** 类器官+免疫/微生物共培养；器官芯片；高通量类器官筛选；prime editing 与表观编辑；体内递送（LNP/AAV）。

**English** — Organoids now span nearly all organs, biobank drug-response data has prospective clinical correlation, and base editing has reached the clinic. Hot: immune/microbial co-culture, organ-on-chip, high-throughput screening, prime and epigenome editing, in-vivo delivery. Gaps: batch heterogeneity and quantitative standards; and **very little work applies editing+organoids to the RNA-regulatory layer rather than to coding genes**. As platforms commoditise, differentiation moves to unique read-outs (reporters) and unique disease models. Blunt: your three items here are **last-author book chapters**. Excellent feasibility evidence — but presenting them as corresponding-author research papers will not survive a reviewer checking the journal type.

### F · 小 RNA 生物学与 miRNA 降解

**中文**
- **进展：** miRNA 生成通路（DROSHA/DGCR8/DICER/AGO）已是教科书内容。近五年领域重心明确转向**miRNA 的降解与末端修饰**：靶标导向的 miRNA 降解（TDMD）由 ZSWIM8–Cul3 泛素连接酶介导（2020 年确立）；TUT4/7 的尿苷化、AGO2 的稳定性构成另一层控制；2026 年 ZSWIM8–AGO2–靶标复合体的冷冻电镜结构问世，机制细节进入结构时代。
- **热点：** TDMD 的生理功能与体内相关性；miRNA 半衰期的组织特异调控；非经典 miRNA 生成；miRNA 治疗（递送仍是瓶颈）。
- **空白（这是最关键的一句）：** **上游信号——激酶、代谢状态、翻译后修饰——如何调控 miRNA 降解机器，几乎是空白。** 绝大多数 TDMD 研究在细胞系里做「机器怎么工作」，很少有人问「机器什么时候被打开」。此外 TDMD 在疾病中的因果作用极少在类器官/大动物层面验证；ZSWIM8 的底物识别「语法」未解。
- **趋势：** 从机制发现走向生理与疾病情境；从细胞系走向组织与体内；信号通路对小 RNA 稳态的控制。
- **对你的位置的判断：** **边缘。** 一篇第 2 作者、零一作。但**那个空白（上游信号 → miRNA 降解）恰好是你 A–E 五条线的交集**——这是本报告最重要的结构性发现。

**English** — Biogenesis is textbook; the field's centre of gravity has moved to **miRNA decay and 3′-end modification**: target-directed miRNA degradation (TDMD) via the ZSWIM8–Cul3 ubiquitin ligase (established 2020), TUT4/7 uridylation, AGO2 stability; a 2026 cryo-EM structure of the ZSWIM8–AGO2–target complex has pushed mechanism into the structural era. Hot: physiological relevance of TDMD, tissue-specific miRNA half-life control, non-canonical biogenesis, miRNA therapeutics. **The decisive gap: how upstream signalling — kinases, metabolic state, post-translational modification — controls the decay machinery is almost unstudied.** Most TDMD work asks how the machine works in cell lines; very few ask when it is switched on. Your position here is **marginal** (one second-author paper, no first-author). But that gap is precisely the intersection of your other five lines — the most important structural finding in this report.

4. **细胞内验证**：AICAR 与 A-769662（**两种机制不同的激动剂互为对照**）+ 葡萄糖剥夺；自制 phospho-S608 抗体检测；AMPKα1/α2 双敲除细胞作遗传对照。
5. **自制抗体**：用你的杂交瘤能力做 phospho-ZSWIM8(S608) 单抗，按 PMID 15728188 的验证范式（磷酸酶处理 + 位点突变体双阴性对照）。

**预期结果（predicted/schematic）** WT + AMP 激活 ≈100%，WT 未激活 ≈44%，S608A ≈9%，无激酶 ≈6%，SAMS 阳性对照 ≈92%。

**陷阱与替代** ① 全长 ZSWIM8（1837 aa）表达困难 → 用片段，但须在细胞内用全长回补验证。② 无序区片段易降解 → 加 MBP/SUMO 标签与蛋白酶抑制剂。③ 若 AMPK 不直接磷酸化 → 检查是否经 SIK/MARK 等 AMPK 家族激酶，或经中间体。

##### 1.4 Aim 2 — 磷酸化是否改变 miRNA 半衰期
**实验**
1. **半衰期测定**：actinomycin D chase + smallRNA-seq，先在已知短命 miRNA 上做阳性对照再上候选（基准数值取自 PMID 31519739、方法取自 PMID 21447562）。
2. **正交方法**：4sU 代谢标记 / SLAM-seq 思路（PMID 28945705）——单一方法的偏倚必须用第二种方法排除。
3. **内源位点突变**：用你的 **ABE/BE4 在内源位点**敲入 S608A（磷酸化缺陷）与 S608D（拟磷酸化），**不用过表达质粒**——审稿人对内源点突变的信任度远高于过表达（可行性见 PMID 31937940 / 31634902 / 33606977）。
4. **候选 miRNA**：miR-29（抗纤维化）、miR-33（胆固醇/AMPK 轴）、miR-375（胰岛）；miR-375 的功能依据见 PMID 15538371。
5. **AGO-RIP** 检查载荷变化（Halo 增强法比 iCLIP 可行，PMID 32497496）。

**预期结果（predicted/schematic）** 降解速率 S608D > WT > S608A > ZSWIM8-KO（k ≈ 0.075 / 0.045 / 0.020 / 0.006 h⁻¹）。

**陷阱与替代** ① Act-D 本身扰动细胞 → 限制在 8 小时内并用 4sU 正交。② 半衰期差异可能被归一化方式掩盖 → 预先固定 spike-in 归一化方案并写进 SOP。③ 若半衰期不变但载荷变化 → 假设改为「磷酸化调控 AGO2 载荷而非降解速率」，方向不废。

##### 1.5 Aim 3 — 在代谢记忆模型里验证因果
**实验** 高糖脉冲后撤除 → 追踪 miR-29/33/375 丰度与靶基因随时间恢复 → 比较 WT / S608A / S608D / ZSWIM8-KO 的「记忆强度」；体系用类器官（你已有）与 T2D 小型猪样本（你自己的一作模型，PMID 见 CV #11）。

**陷阱** 猪样本可及性未确认 → 主线放在类器官，猪作为加分验证。

### 方向 2 · TUT4/7–miR-29 纤维化检查点（最快出成果）
#### Direction 2 · Uridylation checkpoint on anti-fibrotic miRNA

##### 2.1 一句话假设

##### 2.2 科学前提 —— 以及一个必须正面处理的竞争解释
| # | 前提 | 等级 | 依据 |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | miR-29 是跨器官的抗纤维化核心 miRNA | `[已发表]` | 肝 PMID 20890893；肾 21784902；肺 20971881；皮肤/SSc 20201077 |

| **P3** | **miR-29 下降由 TGF-β/Smad3 在转录层驱动** | `[已发表]` | **PMID 21784902、22095944 —— 这是我的降解假设最强的替代解释** |

> **这一条决定方向 2 成立与否，必须写进申请书正面回应：**
> 既然 TGF-β/Smad3 已在转录层解释了 miR-29 下降，我的第一个实验就**不是**测成熟体，而是测 **pri/pre-miR-29 与成熟体的比值**。若前体同步下降 → 转录机制主导，假设降级；若前体不变而成熟体下降 → 降解/加工层被打开，假设成立。**这是一个真正的 go/no-go，而不是一个必然成功的实验。**

##### 2.3 Aim 1 — 区分转录 vs 降解（零成本起步，9 个月内可完成）
**实验** 在你已发表的两个模型的**存档组织**上：① qPCR 测 pri-/pre-/成熟 miR-29a/b/c 三层；② 小 RNA 3′ 末端测序（TAIL-seq 思路，PMID 24582499）测尿苷化比例；③ 与纤维化程度（胶原定量）做相关。
**体系**：MYBPC3 缺失心脏（CV #7）+ SAA3 缺失 DSS-IBD 肠（CV #5）——**两个不同器官，同一逻辑**，这是几乎无人具备的对照。

##### 2.4 Aim 2 — TUT4/7 是否是执行者
**实验** TUT4/TUT7 单敲与双敲（siRNA + ASO 两种方式互为对照）→ miR-29 末端状态与半衰期 → 胶原读出；DIS3L2 敲低验证下游；类器官纤维化模型复现（模型依据 PMID 25828392）。
**关键对照** 必须同时检验「加工层 vs 降解层」：若 TUT4/7 敲低同时改变前体加工，则效应不能归因于降解（依据 P4 的反例）。

##### 2.5 Aim 3 — 可药控性
**实验** ASO 阻断 miR-29 的尿苷化位点 / TUT4-7 抑制剂（若可得）→ 纤维化消退读出；人源验证用狭窄型克罗恩病样本的分子标记对齐（PMID 42622514），病理亚群参照 TWIST1⁺FAP⁺ 成纤维细胞（PMID 39024569）。
**临床语境** IBD 纤维化的未满足需求与终点取自 PMID 27720839（*Gastroenterology* 综述）；**心、肠纤维化目前零获批药**——这是 Significance 的核心数字。

### English summary

Five proposals under one thesis: metabolic and signalling state controlling miRNA decay and 3′-end modification, and thereby the reversibility of chronic disease. Every mechanistic premise is graded `[published]` with a PMID, `[computed in this project]` (the AMPK PSSM — hypothesis-generating only, the score is not evidence), or `[untested]`.

**Direction 1 (flagship)** — AMPK phosphorylates ZSWIM8 to accelerate TDMD of metabolic miRNAs, converting transient metabolic insult into durable memory. Aim 1 is a 12-month go/no-go: in-vitro kinase assay on recombinant disordered-region fragments (WT vs S→A) with a canonical AMPK substrate on the same gel, Phos-tag stoichiometry, MS site localisation above 0.75 probability, and cellular validation with two mechanistically distinct AMPK agonists plus AMPKα1/α2 double knockout. Aim 2 measures half-life by Act-D chase plus an orthogonal 4sU/SLAM-seq approach, with S608A/S608D installed at the **endogenous locus** by base editing rather than overexpression. Aim 3 tests memory strength after a glucose pulse in organoids and T2D pig material. Explicit abandonment condition at 12 months with a named fallback.

**Direction 2 (fastest)** — TUT4/7 uridylation of miR-29 as a druggable fibrosis checkpoint, startable on banked tissue from his own two published organ models at near-zero cost. Crucially, the literature already offers a **strong competing explanation**: TGF-β/Smad3 suppresses miR-29 transcriptionally (PMID 21784902, 22095944). The proposal therefore makes its first experiment the pri/pre-versus-mature ratio, which discriminates the two mechanisms — a genuine go/no-go rather than a guaranteed result. It must also separate the processing layer from the decay layer, because uridylation is known to *promote* processing in other contexts (PMID 23063654, 39054354).

**Direction 3 (identity marker)** — lactate/lactylation as an input to the small-RNA machinery. Precedent is real and recent: lactylation of the RNA-modifying enzymes METTL3 and METTL16 changes their function (PMID 35320754, 37863889), and AARS1 is an established lactate sensor and lactyltransferase (PMID 38653238). No report of lactate acting on the miRNA decay machinery appeared in a 632-paper candidate pool — a genuine gap. Requires a mass-spectrometry collaborator; run it as Aim 3 of Direction 1, not standalone, and control for pH and non-metabolic acidification.

**Direction 4** — an organoid TDMD reporter platform: a resource and collaboration asset rather than an academic identity, differentiated by unique read-out plus his own disease models, with batch CV% reported honestly.

**Direction 5** — base editing to dissect the TDMD grammar at endogenous loci: the mechanistic scalpel that lets Direction 1's key claims rest on endogenous point mutants rather than overexpression, which most RNA labs cannot do.

**Recommended combination:** 1 + 2 as twin core lines sharing the miR-29 narrative, 3 as Aim 3 for differentiation, 4/5 in the feasibility paragraphs. Deliberately exit the lncRNA gastric-cancer line and veterinary virology as standalone directions.

### English field reference

| # | Field | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | One-sentence finding, in my own words | No abstract copying. If you can't write it, you didn't read it. |
| 2 | Which question it answered | Mark whether the question was genuinely open. |
| 3 | Key figure and why it is credible | Figure number + n + quantification method. |
| 4 | Method essentials | Concentrations, durations, cell lines, depth, replicates → copy into Ledger C. |
| 5 | System and limits of extrapolation | How far the claim can travel. |
| 6 | **Controls run and controls omitted** | The omission is usually your entry point. |
| 7 | **Effect size, with numbers** | A note without a number is not a note. |
| 8 | **One thing I do not believe** | Mandatory. Can't find one → read again. |
| 9 | **Hot-topic position** | Foundational / current mainstream / rising / saturating / marginal, plus who is doing it. |
| 10 | **Gaps it exposes or admits** | Copy the authors' own "remains unclear" statements; mark which one *you* could do. |
| 11 | **Where this line goes in three years** | Follow it / avoid it (crowded) / get ahead of it. |
| 12 | Interface to my project | Transferable method, reusable control value, or **competition risk** with a difference statement. |
| 13 | One executable action | "I will do Y in system X, expecting Z" → Ledger A. |
| 14 | Which of its references to queue next | PMIDs into the candidate pool. |

Fields 6, 7 and 8 are what separate a reader from a reviewer; fields 9, 10 and 11 are what you will paste into the Significance and Innovation sections of a grant. Field 12 is how the reading plan stays connected to the bench.

#### 6 · 两个别人想不到、但你独有的技能迁移

1. **杂交瘤单抗 → 自制 phospho-ZSWIM8(S608) 抗体。** 方向 1 最大的技术瓶颈是「没有好的磷酸化抗体」，而这恰好是你 CV 上明确列出的现成能力。做出来之后，这个试剂本身就是你在领域里的护城河与合作货币。**免疫周期 4–6 个月，所以必须在 Q2 启动，不能等到需要时再做。** 验证必须做磷酸酶处理 + S608A 突变体双阴性（PMID 15728188）。
2. **碱基编辑 → 内源位点点突变，而不是过表达。** 审稿人对「内源位点点突变」的信任度远高于过表达质粒，而多数 RNA 生物学实验室不具备这个能力。方向 1 的 Aim 2、方向 3 的 Aim 2、方向 5 的全部，都应该建立在内源编辑之上。这一条能把你的机制结论的可信度整体提高一档。
