### A · 大动物基因编辑与疾病模型

**中文**
- **进展：** CRISPR/Cas9 + 体细胞核移植（SCNT）在猪上已是成熟流水线；从单基因敲除走向精准敲入、碱基编辑（ABE/CBE）、prime editing。多位点编辑猪（去除免疫原/PERV）已推动猪心、猪肾移植进入人体实质阶段（2022 年以来）。
- **热点：** 异种器官移植；猪作为药代与免疫模型；编辑动物的遗传稳定性与生物安全评估；无病毒载体递送。
- **空白：** 大动物脱靶与嵌合体评估无统一标准；疾病模型普遍「建成了但机制没深挖」；成本与世代周期使机制迭代困难；缺少大动物的单细胞/空间图谱。
- **趋势：** **模型工程本身正在服务化、商品化**——做出一个模型的学术溢价在持续下降，价值转移到「用模型回答机制问题」和「作为转化终点的验证」。
- **对你的含义：** 这条线技术含量高、工业界可读性强，但**作为独立 PI 主线已经偏晚**。应定位为工具与终点验证，不作主打。

**English** — Pig CRISPR+SCNT is a mature pipeline; the frontier has moved to precise knock-in, base and prime editing, and multiplex-edited donor pigs now in human xenotransplant trials. Open problems: no standardised off-target/mosaicism assessment in large animals, phenotype-only disease models with shallow mechanism, prohibitive cost per iteration, missing single-cell atlases. The trend that matters for you: **model building itself is commoditising**, so the academic premium has shifted from making a model to answering a mechanistic question with one. Position this as a tool and a translational endpoint, not as your independent line.

### B · 代谢×病毒复制（胆固醇 / 乳酸）

**中文**
- **进展：** 病毒对宿主脂质与胆固醇通路的依赖（复制细胞器膜、脂筏进入）已确立；SREBP/HMGCR 轴、25-羟胆固醇/CH25H 抗病毒轴清楚；感染细胞的 Warburg 样重编程被广泛报道。
- **热点：** 宿主导向抗病毒药物；**乳酸与蛋白乳酰化（lactylation）作为新表观修饰**（2019 年后爆发式增长）；代谢-天然免疫交叉（cGAS/STING、RIG-I 的代谢调控）；脂滴生物学。
- **空白：** **乳酸/乳酰化在 RNA 层面的作用几乎空白**；代谢物如何调控 RNA 稳定性与翻译罕有系统研究；兽医病毒（CSFV/PEDV）的机制发现难以进入主流人类疾病语境。
- **趋势：** 从「代谢为病毒供料」走向「代谢物作为信号，通过乳酰化/巴豆酰化等修饰直接改写宿主机器」。
- **对你的含义：** 你的一作 #8（乳酸→胆固醇→病毒复制）是一条**完整的代谢物因果链**，方法学可以整体迁移到「代谢物→RNA 降解机器」。**但「兽医病毒」这个外壳在北美/欧洲经费体系里是负资产，必须把内核剥出来。**

**English** — Viral dependence on host lipid/cholesterol pathways is established (SREBP/HMGCR, CH25H/25-HC), as is Warburg-like rewiring in infected cells. Hot: host-directed antivirals, and **lactate/protein lactylation as a post-2019 modification field**. The gap that matters: **lactate and lactylation have almost no established role at the RNA level**, and metabolite control of RNA stability is barely studied. Your first-author lactate→cholesterol→replication chain is a complete metabolite-to-phenotype causal argument and transfers wholesale; but the "veterinary virus" packaging is a liability in US/EU funding — extract the core and drop the shell.

### C · 器官纤维化

**中文**
- **进展：** 共同通路清楚（TGF-β/SMAD、YAP/TAZ 机械转导、成纤维细胞→肌成纤维细胞转化）；单细胞图谱已定义病理性成纤维细胞亚群；但**获批抗纤维化药只有 nintedanib 与 pirfenidone，且仅限肺（IPF）——心、肠、肝纤维化零获批药**。
- **热点：** 纤维化的**可逆性与消退（resolution）**；成纤维细胞的代谢重编程（糖酵解/乳酸依赖）；机械力-代谢耦合；器官特异机制 vs 共同机制；类器官与 fibrosis-on-chip 药筛。
- **空白：** **肠纤维化（IBD 狭窄）机制与药物极度缺乏**；抗纤维化 miR-29 已知十余年却未成药——「递送」和「为什么内源 miR-29 在纤维化时被关掉」两个问题都没解；极少有实验室能在**同一套逻辑下横向比较心脏与肠道**纤维化。
- **趋势：** 从抑制活化转向促进消退；靶向成纤维细胞代谢；RNA 疗法（miRNA mimic / antagomiR / ASO）。
- **对你的含义：** **这是你现有资产里领域需求最强、你位置最扎实的一条**——两个不同器官的一作论文 + 自己当 PI 的基金。而「内源 miR-29 为什么被关掉」这个公认空白，恰好是你能用 RNA 降解机制去回答的。

**English** — Core pathways (TGF-β/SMAD, YAP/TAZ, myofibroblast conversion) and pathological fibroblast subsets are well mapped, yet **only two drugs are approved, both lung-only; cardiac, intestinal and hepatic fibrosis have none**. Hot: fibrosis resolution, fibroblast metabolic rewiring, mechano-metabolic coupling, organoid/organ-chip screening. Key gaps: intestinal fibrosis (IBD strictures) is mechanistically and therapeutically bare; **anti-fibrotic miR-29 has been known for over a decade without becoming a drug — neither delivery nor "why is endogenous miR-29 switched off in fibrosis" is solved**; and almost no lab compares heart and gut fibrosis under one framework. This is the strongest-demand, best-owned position among your existing assets — and that open question is exactly one an RNA-decay mechanism can answer.

### E · 类器官与碱基编辑平台

**中文**
- **进展：** 类器官已从肠道扩展到几乎全器官；类器官生物库的药敏结果已有前瞻性临床相关数据；碱基编辑已进入临床（心血管、血液病方向）。
- **热点：** 类器官+免疫/微生物共培养；器官芯片；高通量类器官筛选；prime editing 与表观编辑；体内递送（LNP/AAV）。

**English** — Organoids now span nearly all organs, biobank drug-response data has prospective clinical correlation, and base editing has reached the clinic. Hot: immune/microbial co-culture, organ-on-chip, high-throughput screening, prime and epigenome editing, in-vivo delivery. Gaps: batch heterogeneity and quantitative standards; and **very little work applies editing+organoids to the RNA-regulatory layer rather than to coding genes**. As platforms commoditise, differentiation moves to unique read-outs (reporters) and unique disease models. Blunt: your three items here are **last-author book chapters**. Excellent feasibility evidence — but presenting them as corresponding-author research papers will not survive a reviewer checking the journal type.

### F · 小 RNA 生物学与 miRNA 降解

**中文**
- **进展：** miRNA 生成通路（DROSHA/DGCR8/DICER/AGO）已是教科书内容。近五年领域重心明确转向**miRNA 的降解与末端修饰**：靶标导向的 miRNA 降解（TDMD）由 ZSWIM8–Cul3 泛素连接酶介导（2020 年确立）；TUT4/7 的尿苷化、AGO2 的稳定性构成另一层控制；2026 年 ZSWIM8–AGO2–靶标复合体的冷冻电镜结构问世，机制细节进入结构时代。
- **热点：** TDMD 的生理功能与体内相关性；miRNA 半衰期的组织特异调控；非经典 miRNA 生成；miRNA 治疗（递送仍是瓶颈）。
- **空白（这是最关键的一句）：** **上游信号——激酶、代谢状态、翻译后修饰——如何调控 miRNA 降解机器，几乎是空白。** 绝大多数 TDMD 研究在细胞系里做「机器怎么工作」，很少有人问「机器什么时候被打开」。此外 TDMD 在疾病中的因果作用极少在类器官/大动物层面验证；ZSWIM8 的底物识别「语法」未解。
- **趋势：** 从机制发现走向生理与疾病情境；从细胞系走向组织与体内；信号通路对小 RNA 稳态的控制。
- **对你的位置的判断：** **边缘。** 一篇第 2 作者、零一作。但**那个空白（上游信号 → miRNA 降解）恰好是你 A–E 五条线的交集**——这是本报告最重要的结构性发现。

**English** — Biogenesis is textbook; the field's centre of gravity has moved to **miRNA decay and 3′-end modification**: target-directed miRNA degradation (TDMD) via the ZSWIM8–Cul3 ubiquitin ligase (established 2020), TUT4/7 uridylation, AGO2 stability; a 2026 cryo-EM structure of the ZSWIM8–AGO2–target complex has pushed mechanism into the structural era. Hot: physiological relevance of TDMD, tissue-specific miRNA half-life control, non-canonical biogenesis, miRNA therapeutics. **The decisive gap: how upstream signalling — kinases, metabolic state, post-translational modification — controls the decay machinery is almost unstudied.** Most TDMD work asks how the machine works in cell lines; very few ask when it is switched on. Your position here is **marginal** (one second-author paper, no first-author). But that gap is precisely the intersection of your other five lines — the most important structural finding in this report.
